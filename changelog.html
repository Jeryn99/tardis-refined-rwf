Version 1.20.1-1.0.0
Beta notice:
- This mod is still in beta - things may break or change. Please report bugs and feedback.

New:
- Local Hop: aim at a nearby block and hit the keybind to have the TARDIS dematerialise and rematerialise there. Short cooldown, real travel delay, and a fade animation so it doesn't feel instant.
- Spectator-style flight controls: scroll wheel adjusts flying speed, and look direction affects flight pitch.

Fixed:
- TARDIS/player state not being restored correctly when disconnecting or during a server shutdown mid-flight.
- Local Hop occasionally never completing the teleport.
- Fuel not depleting correctly during some flights.
